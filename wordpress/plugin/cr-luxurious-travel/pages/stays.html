Two Coastlines
Puerto Plata & Cabarete

One collection, two moods.

Cofresí Beach, Puerto Plata is the heart of the collection: ten properties sharing fourteen pools, two beaches, eleven restaurants, two spas and the Ocean World marine park next door. Everything is walkable or a golf-cart ride away.

Cabarete, forty minutes east, is the kite- and windsurfing capital of the Caribbean. Apartment-style suites sit on a golden beach with an ever-present breeze, a rooftop sky lounge and the artisan Brick Oven restaurant.
