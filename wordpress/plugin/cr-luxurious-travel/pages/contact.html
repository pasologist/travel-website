Before You Ask

Common questions.

How does booking through C&R work?

You send an inquiry, an advisor proposes stays and pricing, you confirm, and we secure the reservation along with dining, spa and excursion preferences. You deal with one person throughout.

Are the rates on this site final?

No. The “from” rates are the resorts’ published starting prices per night in USD. Your proposal states the exact rate for your dates, room category and plan, plus any offer that applies.

What happens if I need to change or cancel?

Each rate carries the resorts’ booking conditions, which we state in writing before you confirm. Flexible rates can usually be changed; non-refundable rates cannot.

Can you arrange flights and transfers?

Yes. Ask for a bundled flight-and-hotel quote; private airport transfers are included in every proposal.
