How Dining Works

Reserve once, dine everywhere.

Buffets are walk-in. The specialty restaurants seat by reservation for dinner, and the best tables go early in the week. Tell your C&R advisor which nights matter (a birthday at Azul, a family churrasco at Rodizio) and we will pre-book them before you land.

  • Dress code: smart casual after 6pm
  • Late-night snacks until 4:30am
  • Thirteen bars, from swim-up to the Cellar wine room
  • Dietary needs handled in advance
