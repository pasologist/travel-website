Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Are à la carte restaurants included?

Yes. Dinner at the specialty restaurants is part of the plan; most require a same-day or advance reservation, which your concierge or advisor can arrange.

What is not included?

Spa treatments, excursions off the resort, casino play, motorized water sports and premium wine lists are paid locally or pre-arranged through C&R. We will always tell you in advance.

Do the villas come with the all-inclusive plan?

Villas include a personal chef who prepares meals in your villa, plus VIP access to the resort restaurants and beaches. Your advisor will confirm the exact dining arrangement for your villa and dates.

Is there a dress code?

Casual by day. In the evening, the specialty restaurants ask for smart-casual attire: collared shirts and long trousers for gentlemen, no swimwear.
