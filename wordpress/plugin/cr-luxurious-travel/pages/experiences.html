Beyond the Gates

The north coast, unhurried.

Puerto Plata’s Victorian center and Amber Museum are twenty minutes away; the cable car to Isabel de Torres, ten. Cabarete’s kite beach and Sosúa’s reefs are an easy afternoon. Your advisor can pair excursions with private transfers so you never wait for a bus.
