Privacy notice.

What we collect when you contact us, why we hold it, and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated 6 September 2026.

Who we are

C&R Luxurious Travel is a travel advisory arranging stays at resorts and private villas in the Dominican Republic and, through partner networks, elsewhere. This notice covers the website at crluxurioustravel.com. You can reach us at concierge@crluxurioustravel.com or +1 (914) 481-3073.

What we collect

We collect only what you type into an inquiry form on the Contact or Weddings pages: your name, email address, phone number, the property you are interested in, your travel dates, the number of travelers and your message, together with your consent to be contacted. Nothing on this site asks for payment details, passport numbers or dates of birth.

Our web host also records standard server logs, including your IP address and browser, as part of running and protecting the site.

Why we hold it and for how long

We use your details for one purpose: to answer your inquiry and, if you go on to travel with us, to arrange and support that trip. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a marketing list because you sent an inquiry.

Inquiries are stored in this website's database and emailed to our concierge address. We keep them for as long as we are in conversation with you and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. Ask us and we will delete yours sooner.

Who else is involved

Running the site means a small number of other companies process data on our behalf or receive it as part of loading a page:

  • Hostinger hosts the site and its database.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, which involves a request to Google.
  • Google Maps supplies the embedded map on the Contact page.
  • The resorts' image service serves the photography.
  • Google Site Kit is installed and may connect Google Analytics and Search Console.
  • When you make a booking, the resort or supplier receives the details needed to hold it.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Our caching and security tooling may set functional cookies, and any analytics connected through Site Kit will set its own. Your browser settings control what you accept.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your consent to be contacted. Write to concierge@crluxurioustravel.com and we will respond promptly. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Children

This site is intended for adults planning travel. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children. Where a booking includes children, we receive only what the booking adult chooses to tell us.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and the date at the top.
